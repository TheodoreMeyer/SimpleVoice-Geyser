Your password must be 8–32 characters. Return here and sign in using your Minecraft username and the password you created.

Your SVG password is not your Microsoft, Mojang, or Minecraft account password. Never enter your Microsoft account password here.

Bedrock players can sign in with their gamertag; SVG resolves the Floodgate prefix when the match is unique.
